01 / right now

At AeroCardia I build the sensor characterisation bench for a respiration diagnostics device: firmware that reads motion, pressure and breath-gas sensors on one clock, and the analysis that turns pressure into flow and volume. I am working towards signal processing that runs on the device itself, and towards research in electrophysiology.
