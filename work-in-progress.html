//What I'm doing right now

My capstone is a complete ultrasound imaging platform built from scratch. Five custom boards handle main control, transducer driving, the analog front-end, display and image processing, and power management, and a PyTorch U-Net cleans up the reconstructed image at the end of the chain.

The central constraint is signal integrity. A returning echo is measured in microvolts and every stage between the transducer and the screen can degrade it. That drives most of the design decisions: low-noise analog routing, careful filter design and physical separation between the digital sections and the sensitive front-end. Simulations in k-Wave and ngspice have guided this work, but their results only partially transfer to a physical board, so each stage is validated on hardware as it comes up.

On the imaging side, the U-Net denoiser currently trains on simulated data, which limits how well it generalizes. The next steps are assembling real ultrasound imagery, evaluating against standard image quality metrics and then integrating inference into the platform.
