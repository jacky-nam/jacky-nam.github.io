  • thev

    Tells you whether two electronic components work together, and what it would take to connect them if they don't.

    Parts fail to connect for specific reasons — a 3.3 V board against a 5 V sensor, I2C against SPI, a supply that cannot deliver the current a load wants. thev models each part as a set of typed ports and judges a connection one dimension at a time, so an answer always carries its reason. When two parts do not fit, it searches for the shortest chain of parts that makes them fit.

    Python

Here it is refusing a connection: a USB supply and a 5 V heating pad that agree on direction, protocol, voltage and connector, and fail on current budget alone. The pad wants 1200 mA; the port offers 500. 

$ thev check psu-usb-500ma heater-pad-5v
psu-usb-500ma <-> heater-pad-5v: INCOMPATIBLE
no port pairing satisfies every applicable rule; the closest is out <-> in fails on only 500 mA supplied against 1200 mA drawn

  out <-> in  FAIL
    [pass] direction  source port pairs with sink port
    [pass] protocol   both ports speak power
    [pass] voltage    4.75-5.25 V and 4.75-5.25 V share 4.75-5.25 V
    [FAIL] current    only 500 mA supplied against 1200 mA drawn
    [pass] connector  female usb-a mates with male usb-a
